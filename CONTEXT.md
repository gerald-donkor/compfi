# Compfi commerce

This glossary defines the small catalog vocabulary used by Compfi's storefront.

## Language

**Catalog Product**:
A curated static display record for one distinct piece of furniture. It is not an inventory item or a commercial offer.
_Avoid_: SKU, listing, item

**Product Category**:
One of Compfi's three browse groupings: dining, living, or bedroom.
_Avoid_: collection, department

**Product Media**:
The local visual record associated with a Catalog Product, including its path, intrinsic dimensions, focal guidance, and descriptive alternative text.
_Avoid_: asset URL, image metadata

**Display Price**:
A non-negative USD amount represented as an integer number of cents for presentation only.
_Avoid_: sale price, offer
